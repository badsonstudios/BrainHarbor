A tumor board is a meeting where specialists look at one person's case
together: surgeons, cancer doctors, the lab, and the people who read scans. It
tends to happen when a case is complicated or something is hard to read.

You do not go to the meeting. Afterwards, the doctor who knows you best
explains what was said.

What they decide is advice, not an order. The choice is still yours. Because
you are not in the room, tell your team what matters to you before it happens,
so it is taken into account.
