**If the tumor is in the spinal cord, or has spread to the spine, new signs from
the cord are their own rule.** For you, an arm or a leg that is newly weak, or
weaker than it was, is a right-away call, not a same-day one.

So are new trouble walking, new numbness or tingling, new or worse pain in the
back or neck, and new trouble controlling the bladder or bowel. When cancer
presses on the spinal cord, Cancer Research UK calls it an emergency that needs
treating quickly. Call your team right away, at any hour, or go to the emergency
department.
