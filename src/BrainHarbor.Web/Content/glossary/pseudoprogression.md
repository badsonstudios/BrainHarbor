---
term: pseudoprogression
pronunciation: SOO-doh-pro-GRESH-un
sources:
  - url: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10412732/
    title: "Pseudoprogression versus true progression in glioblastoma"
    accessed: 2026-08-30
---

A scan after treatment that looks worse because of the treatment itself, not
because the tumor has grown. Telling the two apart can take another scan.
