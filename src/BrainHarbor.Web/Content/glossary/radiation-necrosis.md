---
term: radiation necrosis
pronunciation: neh-KROH-sis
sources:
  - url: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8507553/
    title: "Risk factors for radiation necrosis after cranial stereotactic radiosurgery"
    accessed: 2026-08-30
---

Damaged tissue where radiation was aimed, months or years later. On a scan it
can look like tumor coming back. Telling the two apart can take more scans, and
sometimes a sample.
