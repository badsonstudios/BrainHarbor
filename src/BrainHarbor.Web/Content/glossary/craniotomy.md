---
term: craniotomy
pronunciation: kray-nee-OT-uh-mee
sources:
  - url: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560922/
    title: "Craniotomy (StatPearls)"
    accessed: 2026-08-30
---

An operation that opens a window in the skull to reach the brain. The piece of
bone is put back at the end and fixed in place.
