---
term: hydrocephalus
pronunciation: hy-droh-SEF-uh-lus
sources:
  - url: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560875/
    title: "Hydrocephalus (StatPearls)"
    accessed: 2026-08-30
---

Fluid building up inside the brain because it cannot drain the usual way. It
raises the pressure in the skull. There are treatments to let it drain again.
