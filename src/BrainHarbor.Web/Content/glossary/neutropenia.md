---
term: neutropenia
also: [low white blood cell count]
pronunciation: noo-troh-PEE-nee-uh
sources:
  - url: https://www.cancer.org/cancer/managing-cancer/side-effects/low-blood-counts/neutropenia.html
    title: "American Cancer Society: Low White Blood Cell Counts (Neutropenia)"
    accessed: 2026-09-04
---

Having too few of the white blood cells that fight infection. It is a common
effect of chemotherapy, and it is why a temperature has to be reported at once.
