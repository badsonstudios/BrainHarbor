---
term: nadir
pronunciation: NAY-deer
sources:
  - url: https://www.cancer.org/cancer/managing-cancer/side-effects/low-blood-counts/neutropenia.html
    title: "American Cancer Society: Low White Blood Cell Counts (Neutropenia)"
    accessed: 2026-09-04
---

The lowest point your blood counts reach after a dose of chemotherapy. It is
usually about a week to ten days afterwards, and it is when infection is most
of a risk.
