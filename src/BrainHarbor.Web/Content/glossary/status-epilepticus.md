---
term: status epilepticus
pronunciation: STAY-tus ep-ih-LEP-tih-kus
sources:
  - url: https://www.epilepsy.com/complications-risks/emergencies/status-epilepticus
    title: "Epilepsy Foundation: What Is Status Epilepticus?"
    accessed: 2026-08-30
---

A seizure that lasts more than five minutes. Or seizures one after another, with
no waking up in between. This one is an emergency. Call an ambulance.
