---
term: tonic-clonic seizure
also: [grand mal]
pronunciation: TON-ik KLON-ik
sources:

    accessed: 2026-08-30
---

The kind of seizure most people picture. The muscles stiffen, then the arms and
legs jerk, and the person is not awake for it. Most last one to three minutes.
An older name is grand mal.
