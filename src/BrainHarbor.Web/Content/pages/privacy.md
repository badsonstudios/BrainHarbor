## The short version

We do not track you. We do not run ads. We do not sell anything about you to
anyone, ever.

## What we collect

**When you read the site:** nothing that names you. We do not run analytics
and we do not build a profile of you.

**When you change text size:** a small cookie remembers your choice for a
year. It holds one word, large or standard, and nothing else.

**When you search for trials near you:** you can type a ZIP code, or let your
browser share your location. We turn it into a point on the map and send that
point to ClinicalTrials.gov to ask which trials have a site nearby. We do not
save it, and we do not write it to our logs. It is gone when the page finishes
loading. You never have to share your location: typing a ZIP works just as
well, and browsing the list works without either.

**When the digest opens:** we will ask for your email address, and nothing
else. Sign-up is not open yet, so we hold no addresses today.

## What we do not do

- No ad networks. No tracking pixels. No third-party analytics that follow
  you to other sites.
- No selling, renting, or sharing your email.
- We never ask for your diagnosis, your medical records, or your date of
  birth.

## Your email, when the digest opens

You will confirm your address before we send anything. Every email will have
a link to stop. When you stop, we delete your address rather than keep you on
a list.

## Links to other sites

Some links go to other organizations, like the National Cancer Institute.
Those sites have their own rules. We do not control them.

## Changes

If these promises change, we will say so on this page and note the date.
