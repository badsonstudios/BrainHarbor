## What it is

Once a week we send one short email. It has the few research updates from
that week that matter most for patients and families, in plain words.

## What it is not

It is not a news blast. Most weeks, real progress is small. We would rather
send you five honest lines than a page of noise.

## Our promises about your email

- We never sell or share your address.
- We never send ads.
- Every email has a one-click way to stop.
- If you leave, we delete your address.

## Signing up

Sign-up opens soon. When it does, you will confirm by clicking a link in your
first email, so nobody can add you without your say-so.
