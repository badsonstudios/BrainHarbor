## What a diffuse midline glioma is

This is a %%glioma%% that grows in the middle parts of the brain or in the
spinal cord. Middle parts include the brain stem, the thalamus, and the cord
itself.

Diffuse means it spreads into healthy tissue around it instead of sitting in
one lump. That makes it hard to remove with surgery.

It is graded 4.

## The gene change in its name

Most of these tumors have a change called **H3 K27**. Your report may say
"H3 K27-altered". Doctors use that change to name the tumor, not just where it
sits.

A tumor in the pons, a part of the brain stem, may be called %%DIPG%%. DIPG is
one kind of diffuse midline glioma, not another word for all of them.

## What to ask your team

- Where exactly is mine?
- Was it tested for the H3 K27 change?
- Can a biopsy be done safely, and would it change the plan?
