## What DIPG is

DIPG stands for diffuse intrinsic pontine glioma. It is a tumor in the pons,
which is part of the brain stem.

The brain stem controls breathing, heartbeat, swallowing, and eye movement. A
tumor there causes symptoms early, and it sits among parts the body cannot do
without.

DIPG is a kind of %%diffuse midline glioma%%. It grows into the tissue around
it rather than staying in one lump, so surgery cannot remove it.

It is most often found in children.

## Reading research about it

Some studies say DIPG, some say diffuse midline glioma, and they are not always
about the same group of people. DIPG is the pontine kind. A study of midline
gliomas may include tumors elsewhere.

## What to ask your team

- Is the tumor in the pons, or another midline place?
- What is treatment meant to do?
- Are there trials open for this, and would we qualify?
