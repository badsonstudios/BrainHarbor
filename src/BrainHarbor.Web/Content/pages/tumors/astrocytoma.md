## What an astrocytoma is

Some helper cells in the brain are shaped like stars. They are called
astrocytes. An astrocytoma is a %%glioma%% that starts in those cells.

Astrocytomas run from slow growing to fast growing, so the grade on your report
matters as much as the name.

## Kinds you may see

**Pilocytic astrocytoma** is usually grade 1 and grows slowly. It is more common
in children and young adults.

**IDH-mutant astrocytoma** has an %%IDH gene change%%. It can be grade 2, 3 or
4. A grade 4 IDH-mutant astrocytoma is not the same tumor as
%%glioblastoma%%, even though both are grade 4.

## What to ask your team

- What grade is mine?
- Was it tested for an IDH gene change?
- Can surgery reach where it sits?
