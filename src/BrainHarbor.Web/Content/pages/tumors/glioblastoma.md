## What glioblastoma is

Glioblastoma is a %%glioma%% that grows fast. Doctors grade brain tumors from
1 to 4, and glioblastoma is grade 4. It is the most common brain tumor that
starts in the brain in adults.

It sends thin threads of tumor cells into the brain around it. That is why
surgery alone does not usually get all of it, even when the scan looks clear
afterward.

## Words you may see with it

Your report may say **IDH-wildtype**. Under the rules doctors use now, a tumor
must be IDH-wildtype to be called glioblastoma. A grade 4 tumor with an
%%IDH gene change%% is given a different name, and it behaves differently.

You may also see **MGMT**. That is a test on the tumor that can help your team
choose treatment.

Some older papers say "glioblastoma multiforme" or GBM. That is the same tumor.

## What to ask your team

- Was my tumor tested for IDH and MGMT?
- Where in my brain is it, and can surgery reach it?
- What is the plan, and in what order?
