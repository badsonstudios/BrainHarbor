## What an oligodendroglioma is

An oligodendroglioma is a %%glioma%% that starts in helper cells called
oligodendrocytes. These cells wrap nerve fibres and help signals move.

## The two tests that name it

To call a tumor an oligodendroglioma, doctors look for two things in the tumor
cells:

- an %%IDH gene change%%, and
- a change called **1p/19q co-deletion**, where parts of two chromosomes are
  missing.

Both must be there. If your report names this tumor, those tests were done.

Seizures are a common first sign, often years before anything else shows up.

## What to ask your team

- Did my tumor have both changes?
- What grade is it?
- What are we watching for over time?
