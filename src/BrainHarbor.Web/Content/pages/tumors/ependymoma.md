## What an ependymoma is

Your brain and spinal cord have spaces filled with fluid. Those spaces are
lined with cells called ependymal cells. An ependymoma starts in that lining.

Because it grows in the fluid spaces, it can block the flow of fluid. That
build-up causes many of the early symptoms, like headaches or feeling sick.

## Where it turns up

An ependymoma can grow in the brain or in the spinal cord. It happens in both
children and adults, and where it sits often differs between them.

Doctors also look at gene changes in the tumor now, because two ependymomas
that look alike under a microscope can behave differently.

## What to ask your team

- Where is mine, and is it blocking fluid?
- What grade is it?
- Was it tested for gene changes?
