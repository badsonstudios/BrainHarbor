## What this means

This is not one tumor. It is a way to group research about brain tumors in
children.

## Why children are not small adults here

The types are different. %%Medulloblastoma%% and some midline tumors are far
more common in children, while %%glioblastoma%% and %%brain metastases%% are
far more common in adults.

The same tumor name can also behave differently in a child than in an adult,
and be treated differently.

And a child's brain is still growing, so care teams weigh the effects of
treatment on learning, hearing and growth for years afterwards. That is why
children's teams plan long follow-up from the start.

## Reading research with this filter on

A study in adults may not apply to a child even when the tumor name matches.
Check who was in the study before you carry the result across.

## What to ask your team

- Is my child's care led by a children's brain tumor team?
- Was this research done in children?
- Who follows growth, learning and hearing later on?
