## What a meningioma is

Your brain is wrapped in thin layers called the meninges. A meningioma starts
in those layers, not in the brain itself. It grows on the outside and can press
inward.

Most meningiomas are grade 1 and grow slowly. Some are grade 2 or 3 and grow
faster.

## What often happens

Many are found by accident, on a scan done for another reason. A small one that
causes no trouble may just be watched with scans.

Because a meningioma sits on the outside, symptoms often come from where it
presses rather than from the tumor itself.

## What to ask your team

- What grade is mine?
- Is it pressing on anything that explains my symptoms?
- Are we watching it or treating it, and why?
