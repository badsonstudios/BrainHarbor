## What brain metastases are

These are tumors in the brain that did not start there. Cancer cells travelled
from somewhere else in the body and settled in the brain.

You may hear them called brain mets, or secondary brain tumors.

## Why the starting point matters

A brain metastasis is still made of the first cancer. Lung cancer that spreads
to the brain is treated as lung cancer in the brain, not as a brain tumor that
started there.

That is why your team keeps naming the first cancer, and why treatment often
follows what works for that cancer.

There can be one, or several in different places. Your team counts them and
notes where they are, because that shapes the plan.

## What to ask your team

- Where did this cancer start?
- How many are there, and where?
- Does my treatment need to reach the brain specifically?
