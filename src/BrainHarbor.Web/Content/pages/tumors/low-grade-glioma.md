## What a low-grade glioma is

A low-grade %%glioma%% grows slowly. Doctors grade brain tumors from 1 to 4.
Low grade means grade 1 or grade 2.

Slow does not mean harmless. Where a tumor sits can matter as much as how fast
it grows, because it presses on the part of the brain around it.

## What often happens

Because these grow slowly, a care team may watch a small one with scans for a
while before doing anything. That plan has a name: active surveillance. Being
watched is a real plan, not a delay.

Some low-grade gliomas change over time and start growing faster. That is why
your team keeps checking.

## What to ask your team

- What grade is mine, and what does that mean for me?
- Are we watching it, or treating it now, and why?
- What would make us change the plan?
